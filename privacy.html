Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Your files

Files you open in a ToolSprint tool are processed on your own device, inside your browser. They are not uploaded to ToolSprint or to any third party.

Text you type

Text typed into tools (for example the Word Counter or JSON Formatter) stays in your browser and is not sent anywhere.

Accounts and cookies

ToolSprint has no accounts or sign-up. The site does not set its own tracking cookies.

Advertising and analytics

ToolSprint may show advertising and use basic analytics in future. If that happens, this page will be updated first to list the providers, the cookies they use and how to opt out.

Third-party hosting

Like any website, the host that serves these pages may keep standard server logs such as IP address and page requested.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to us through the contact page.
