Text to Speech

Paste a script, article or caption and hear it read aloud in your browser's own voice — useful for proof-listening your own writing.

Speech is generated by your own device/browser voices. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.

How to use

1Paste your text
A paragraph at a time reads most naturally.
2Pick a voice & speed
The voice list comes from your device — it differs per phone/PC.
3Listen
Press Listen; long text keeps playing until it ends or you stop it.
4Stop any time
The Stop button cuts speech immediately.

Frequently asked questions

Why is the voice list empty or different on my phone?

Voices are supplied by your operating system and browser, not by ToolSprintHub. Android, iPhone, Windows and Mac all ship different sets; the Default voice always works even if the list looks empty at first.

Can I download the speech as an audio file?

No — the browser speech engine plays audio live but does not offer a recording to save. For a downloadable voiceover you need a dedicated TTS service.

Is there a length limit?

Some browsers pause or cut off very long passages (Chrome desktop notoriously stops around 15 seconds on remote voices). Splitting your text into paragraphs and playing them one at a time avoids it.
