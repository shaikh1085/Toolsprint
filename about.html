About ToolSprint

ToolSprint is a free collection of 50 everyday tools: PDF tools, image tools, text and developer helpers, generators, calculators and timers. Every tool lives on its own page, opens instantly and does one job well.

How it works

Every tool runs inside your browser. When you merge a PDF or compress a photo, the work happens on your own phone or computer. Your files are processed in your browser and never uploaded to a server.

What you get

  • Free: no sign-up, no trial, no watermark on anything you download.
  • Private: nothing you open is sent to ToolSprint.
  • Fast: light pages with no heavy frameworks.
