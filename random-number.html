Random Number Generator

Pick random numbers between any minimum and maximum — for draws, games, sampling and decisions — with an optional mode that guarantees no repeats.

How to use

1Set the range
Minimum and maximum, both included in the draw.
2Choose how many
One for a decision, several for a draw or sample.
3Optional: no repeats
Tick it for raffles and lotteries where duplicates are not allowed.
4Pick & copy
Results appear in draw order; copy them with one click.

Frequently asked questions

How random are these numbers?

They come from your browser's cryptographically secure random source (the same one used for security tokens), mapped evenly into your range — no patterns, no favouritism toward any number.

Why did it refuse my no-repeats request?

Because it was impossible: with no repeats, you cannot pick more numbers than the range contains. Picking 10 unique numbers from 1–5 has no answer, so the tool explains instead of looping forever.

Are the minimum and maximum themselves possible results?

Yes — the range is inclusive on both ends, which is what most draws and games expect.
