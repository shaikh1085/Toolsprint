Frequently asked questions

Where do Celsius and Fahrenheit cross?

At −40: −40 °C and −40 °F are the same temperature — a handy sanity check for any converter, including this one.

Why can Kelvin not be negative?

Kelvin starts at absolute zero (0 K = −273.15 °C), the coldest physically possible temperature, so a negative Kelvin value is an input error and the tool will tell you.

What are the everyday reference points?

Water freezes at 0 °C / 32 °F and boils at 100 °C / 212 °F; body temperature is about 37 °C / 98.6 °F; a hot day is 35 °C / 95 °F.
