Frequently asked questions

How is percent change different from “what percent”?

“X is what % of Y” compares two numbers at one moment (50 is 25% of 200). Percent change compares a value over time: from 100 to 150 is a +50% change. Mixing them up is the most common percentage mistake — this tool keeps them as separate modes.

Why can't it compute % change from 0?

Percent change divides by the starting value, and dividing by zero is undefined — going from 0 to anything is not a finite percentage. The tool says this plainly instead of showing Infinity%.

For a discount, does it show the amount saved too?

Yes — Discount mode shows both the sale price and how much you save, since that is usually the number you actually want.
