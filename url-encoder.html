Frequently asked questions

Does this encode a whole URL or just a component?

Just a component — it escapes everything structural too, so a full URL's : / ? and & would be encoded. That is the right behaviour for a query value or path segment. To keep a whole URL's structure intact, encode only the part you are adding to it.

Why did my space become %20 and not +?

Both exist: + means space only inside form-encoded query strings, while %20 works everywhere in a URL. This tool uses %20, the universally safe choice.

Decode showed an error. What is wrong?

Probably a bare % that is not followed by two hex digits, or a truncated escape at the end. The error message appears instead of a half-decoded result so you can find and fix the bad spot.
