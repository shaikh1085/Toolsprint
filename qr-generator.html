Frequently asked questions

Do QR codes from here expire?

No. The code simply encodes your text — there is no ToolSprintHub redirect in between — so it works for as long as the link or Wi-Fi details stay the same.

Why doesn't my Wi-Fi QR code connect?

Check the SSID and password character-for-character (they are case-sensitive) and that the security type matches your router. The tool escapes special characters in the standard WIFI: format, but a typo in the password is the usual cause.

How big should I print a QR code?

At least 2×2 cm for close-up scanning, and bigger the further away the scanner will be — roughly 1:10 code-size to scanning-distance.

Is it safe to put my Wi-Fi password in a QR code?

Anyone who scans or photographs it gets the password, so treat the printed code like the password itself — fine for your living room, not for a public noticeboard.
