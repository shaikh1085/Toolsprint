Frequently asked questions

What is Sentence case exactly?

Only the first letter of each sentence is capitalised — like normal writing. This converter lowercases everything first, then capitalises after each full stop, question mark or exclamation mark, so “hELLO. hOW are YOU?” becomes “Hello. How are you?”

Will Title Case capitalise every word?

It capitalises the first letter of every word, including small words like “and” and “the”. Some style guides leave those lowercase — check the result if you follow one.

What does camelCase do with spaces and punctuation?

It removes spaces and punctuation, lowercases the first word and capitalises each following word: “my video title” becomes “myVideoTitle”. It is meant for variable and file names in code.
