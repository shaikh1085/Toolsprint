Frequently asked questions

Why do I get both #babytalk and #baby #talk?

Because both forms get searched. The joined tag targets the exact topic; the split tags catch broader searches. The generator always emits them as separate hashtags — never one glued-together string.

How many hashtags should I actually post?

Platform guidance shifts, but a focused set of roughly 5–12 relevant tags is the common sweet spot. This tool gives you a set to choose from, not a quota to fill.

Do hashtags guarantee more views?

No — they help the platform categorise your post, nothing more. The first two seconds of the video and watch time matter far more than any tag.
