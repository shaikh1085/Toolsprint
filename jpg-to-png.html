Frequently asked questions

Does converting to PNG improve the photo's quality?

No. PNG preserves exactly the pixels the JPG already has — it cannot restore detail the JPG compression removed. The benefit is that future edits and re-saves lose nothing more.

Why is the PNG so much bigger?

JPG throws away detail to stay small; PNG stores every pixel. Photo PNGs are commonly 3–8× the JPG size, and the tool warns you before you download.

Can this add transparency to my JPG?

No — a JPG has no transparent areas to recover. The PNG starts fully opaque; removing a background needs a dedicated editor.
