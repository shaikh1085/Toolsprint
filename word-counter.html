Frequently asked questions

How are sentences counted?

By sentence-ending punctuation (. ! ?) — and a final sentence without a full stop still counts, so a one-line caption is 1 sentence, not 0.

How is reading time worked out?

At an average adult reading speed of about 200 words per minute, rounded to the nearest minute (anything under ~30 seconds shows as “< 1 min”).

Do numbers and hyphenated words count as words?

Numbers count as words, matching most editors. A hyphenated word like “well-known” counts as one word, since it has no spaces.

Is my text saved anywhere?

No. Counting happens in this tab only; reload the page and the text is gone. Keep your own copy of anything important.
