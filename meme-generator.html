Frequently asked questions

Why does long text overflow the image?

The caption size is fixed relative to the image width, so very long lines can run off the edge. Keep each line under roughly 40 characters — memes are punchier short anyway.

Is there really no watermark?

Really none. What you see in the preview is exactly the file you download.

Can I use any image I find online?

Technically yes, but the rights are yours to check — only caption images you own, made yourself, or know are free to reuse.
