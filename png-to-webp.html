Frequently asked questions

Does WebP keep transparency?

Yes — unlike JPG, WebP supports an alpha channel, so transparent PNG logos and cut-outs stay transparent after conversion.

How much smaller will my image be?

Typically 25–50% smaller than the same PNG at similar quality. Flat graphics with few colours save the most; noisy photos save less.

Is WebP supported everywhere?

All modern browsers (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari 14+) display WebP. Very old software and some print workflows still want PNG or JPG — keep your original PNG as the master copy.
