Frequently asked questions

Which copy of a repeated line is kept?

The first one, in its original position. Order is otherwise untouched — this tool never re-sorts your list (use Sort Lines for that).

What happens to blank lines?

They are removed entirely — a blank line in the output would just be noise. Your result is one item per line, nothing else.

“Apple” vs “apple ” — are they duplicates?

Only if you tick the options: “Ignore surrounding spaces” treats “apple ” as “apple”, and “Ignore case” treats “Apple” as “apple”. With both off, lines must match exactly.
