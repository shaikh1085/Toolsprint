Frequently asked questions

Does the count include both the first and last day?

No — it is the number of full days between the dates, the standard convention: 1 Jan to 2 Jan is 1 day. If you need an inclusive count for a booking or rental, add 1 to the day total.

What if I enter the dates in reverse order?

You still get the same span — the tool takes the absolute difference and tells you which date is earlier, rather than showing a negative number.

How are months counted in the breakdown?

As calendar months: from 15 Jan to 15 Mar is exactly 2 months, and the leftover days are counted after the last full month — not as a flat 30-day conversion.
