Frequently asked questions

Is Base64 encryption?

No. Base64 is just an encoding — anyone can decode it instantly, including with this very tool. Never use it to “protect” passwords or secrets; use real encryption for that.

Why does encoded text end with = signs?

Padding. Base64 works in 3-byte groups; when the input does not divide evenly, = characters fill the last group so decoders know exactly where the data ends.

Does it handle Urdu, Arabic and emoji?

Yes. The text is converted to UTF-8 bytes before encoding (and back after decoding), so non-Latin scripts and emoji survive the round trip — unlike naive browser btoa calls, which throw errors on them.
