Frequently asked questions

What size should I use for Instagram or YouTube?

Common sizes: Instagram post 1080×1080, Instagram/Reels cover 1080×1920, YouTube thumbnail 1280×720, profile photos are usually square — 800×800 is plenty.

Will making an image bigger improve its quality?

No. Enlarging stretches the pixels that already exist, so a small image made bigger will look soft. Resizing down is where this tool shines.

Why does the output come out as PNG?

PNG keeps the resized result lossless. If you need a smaller JPG afterwards, run the PNG through the Image Compressor.

What happens if I untick “keep aspect ratio”?

You can set any width and height independently, but the picture may look stretched or squashed. The preview shows exactly what you will get.
