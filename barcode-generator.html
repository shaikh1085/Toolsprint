Frequently asked questions

Why CODE128 and not EAN or UPC?

EAN/UPC numbers are registered product codes with check digits, issued by GS1 for retail. CODE128 encodes anything without registration, which is right for internal labels, assets, tickets and warehouse use — but shops cannot scan it at checkout as a product price code.

How small can I print it?

Keep the printed barcode at least ~3 cm wide for short values, with clear white margins. Longer text makes a wider barcode — shrinking a wide barcode too small is the top reason scans fail.

Can scanners read it from the screen?

Yes, most phone scanner apps and handheld scanners read the preview directly — a handy way to test before you print a batch of labels.
