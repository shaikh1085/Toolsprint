2Going to a timestamp?

3Seconds vs milliseconds

Frequently asked questions

What exactly is a Unix timestamp?

The number of seconds since 1 January 1970, 00:00:00 UTC — a single number every computer on earth agrees on, with no time zones or daylight-saving confusion. That is why logs, APIs and databases store it.

Why do I see two date results?

The same instant in UTC and in your local time zone. They are the same moment — the clock reading differs because time zones shift the displayed hour.

My timestamp gave a date in 1970 or the year 56000. What's wrong?

A unit mix-up: milliseconds fed in as seconds land ~1000× too far in the future, and seconds treated as milliseconds land near 1970. This tool auto-detects by digit count, so check you pasted the whole number.
