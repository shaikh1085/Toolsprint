Frequently asked questions

Could two generated UUIDs ever collide?

Practically no. A v4 UUID has 122 random bits — you would need to generate billions per second for centuries before a collision became likely. That is the entire point of the format.

Why does the third group always start with 4?

That digit is the version marker: 4 means “randomly generated”. The first digit of the fourth group is likewise always 8, 9, a or b (the variant marker) — seeing them tells you the UUID is well-formed.

Uppercase or lowercase?

This tool outputs lowercase, the most common convention. Most systems accept either, since UUID comparison is case-insensitive in practice.
