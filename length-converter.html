Frequently asked questions

How many centimetres are in an inch, exactly?

Exactly 2.54 — that has been the international definition since 1959, and it is the factor this converter uses for all imperial-to-metric conversions.

Can it do feet-and-inches, like 5′ 9″?

Convert the parts separately and add them: 5 feet plus 9 inches converted to centimetres, summed, gives your height. Or convert 69 inches (5×12+9) in one go — same answer.

How is this different from the Unit Converter?

It is the length-only specialist: a shorter unit list and a bigger, faster workflow when length is all you need. The full Unit Converter also covers weight, volume, area, speed and data.
