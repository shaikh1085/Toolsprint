Frequently asked questions

How are the months and days worked out?

Calendar-accurately: full years first, then full calendar months, then the remaining days — the same method official age checks use, so a person born on 31 January ages correctly across short Februaries.

Can I calculate age for a future date?

Yes — that is what the second date field is for. Age at a school admission cut-off or a future birthday works exactly the same way.

What if the birth date is after the “as of” date?

The tool stops with a clear error instead of showing a negative age — most likely the dates were entered the wrong way round.
