How to use

1Add your image
Drag & drop it onto the dashed box, or click the box and pick a JPG or PNG. The background colour is detected from the corners automatically.
2Remove
Press Remove background. The result appears on a checkerboard, which shows the transparent areas.
3Fine-tune
Background left over? Raise Tolerance. Subject being eaten? Lower it — or click the background in the Original preview to sample its exact colour.
4Download
Save the transparent PNG, ready for designs, listings and documents.

Frequently asked questions

What kinds of images work best with this background remover?

Images with a plain or solid background: product photos on white, logos, scanned documents and portraits shot against a plain wall. The tool removes the colour connected to the edges of the picture, so a busy background — trees, crowds, patterned wallpaper — will only come off in patches. For those photos a server-based AI remover is still better; this one is honest about being a plain-background tool.

Part of my subject disappeared, or some background is left. How do I fix it?

Adjust the Tolerance slider: lower it if parts of the subject are being removed, raise it if background is left behind. If the background is not one even colour, click directly on the background in the Original preview to sample its exact colour, then remove again. Switching Mode to “Everywhere” helps for logos and graphics where the same background colour also appears inside the picture.

Is my image uploaded to a server?

No. The removal happens entirely in your browser using your own device — there is no upload, no account and no copy of your image anywhere else. That is also why it works on plain backgrounds rather than using a server AI model.

Why is the download a PNG, and why might it be resized?

PNG is the download format because JPG cannot store transparency. Very large photos are scaled down to at most 2048 pixels on the longest side before processing, so the tool stays fast and does not freeze your phone; the size line under the result always shows the exact output dimensions.
