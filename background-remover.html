How to use

1Add your image
Drag & drop it onto the dashed box, or click the box and pick a JPG or PNG.
2Pick the mode
Smart AI for photos of people with a real background behind them — best with 1–2 people and a clear subject, and it needs internet the first time to download the model. Plain background for logos, graphics and product shots on one flat colour.
3Remove & fine-tune
Press Remove background. In Smart AI, use Detail and Edge softness to clean up hair and outlines — check the edge on the white and black previews. In Plain mode, use Tolerance.
4Download
Save the transparent PNG, ready for designs, listings, thumbnails and documents.

Frequently asked questions

Which mode should I use — Smart AI or Plain background?

Use Smart AI for photos of people — selfies and portraits with a busy background like sky, trees, fountains, streets or rooms. It needs an internet connection the first time, to download its model to your browser; after that it runs on your device. It works best on photos with 1–2 people and a clear subject. Use Plain background mode for logos, graphics and product photos shot on one flat colour: it removes the colour connected to the edges of the picture, which keeps flat artwork crisp.

The edges look rough, or some hair is missing. How do I fix it?

In Smart AI mode, lower the Detail slider to bring back fine edges like hair, or raise it if bits of background are clinging to the outline. Raise Edge softness for a smoother, less jagged cut, and preview against the white and black backgrounds to check the edge from both sides. In Plain mode, adjust Tolerance instead: lower it if parts of the subject are being removed, raise it if background is left behind.

Is my image uploaded to a server?

No. Both modes run entirely in your browser on your own device — there is no upload, no account and no copy of your image anywhere else. Smart AI mode downloads a small AI model to your browser the first time you use it (a few MB, so the first removal needs an internet connection); after that the model works on your photo locally, just like the plain mode.

Why is the download a PNG, and why might it be resized?

PNG is the download format because JPG cannot store transparency. Very large photos are scaled down to at most 2048 pixels on the longest side before processing, so the tool stays fast and does not freeze your phone; the size line under the result always shows the exact output dimensions.
