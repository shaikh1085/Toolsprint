Frequently asked questions

What happens to transparency when I convert PNG to JPG?

JPG has no transparency, so this tool fills the transparent area with white before saving — your logo or cut-out will not end up on a black background.

Which format makes the smallest file?

For photos, WebP is usually smallest, then JPG. PNG is best for screenshots, logos and anything with sharp edges or transparency, but photo PNGs can be several times larger than the same image as JPG.

Can I convert a WebP back to PNG?

Yes — WebP to PNG is fully supported and lossless from the WebP's decoded pixels. Note that converting JPG to PNG cannot restore quality the JPG already lost.

Why did the converted file come out bigger?

Converting a compressed format (JPG/WebP) into PNG stores every pixel, so PNG output is often larger. The tool shows both sizes and warns you before you download.
