Frequently asked questions

How accurate is the stopwatch?

It measures from the system clock rather than counting screen updates, so even if the display refresh is throttled in a background tab, the elapsed time stays correct to within a tenth of a second.

Will the countdown beep if the tab is in the background?

The countdown itself stays accurate, but some browsers hold sound until the tab becomes visible — the display will already show 00:00 and the “time's up” message when you return.

Can both timers run at once?

Yes — the stopwatch and countdown are independent. Timing a race while a presentation countdown runs is exactly the sort of thing this page is for.
