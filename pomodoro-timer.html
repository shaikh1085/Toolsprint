How to use

1Pick one task
One pomodoro, one job — that is the method.
2Start the focus timer
25 minutes. No messages, no tabs, just the task.
3Take the 5-minute break
When it beeps, stop properly — stand up, water, look away.
4Repeat
After four focus sessions, take a longer break of 15–30 minutes away from the timer.

Frequently asked questions

What if I finish the task mid-pomodoro?

Use the remaining minutes to review or improve the work, then stop the sprint early with Reset if you like. The classic method says the pomodoro is indivisible — but finished is finished.

Why 25 minutes specifically?

It is the original length from Francesco Cirillo's technique: long enough to make real progress, short enough that starting feels easy. The break length matters as much as the focus length — do not skip it.

Does the timer keep running if I switch tabs?

Yes. The countdown is based on the clock, not on tab visibility, so it stays accurate in a background tab — though browsers may delay the beep until the tab is visible again.
