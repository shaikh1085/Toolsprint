PDF Split

Pull a page range out of a PDF — for example pages 3–7 of a report — and save just those pages as a new, smaller PDF.

Drag & drop a PDF here, or choose one file.

Private: Your file is processed in your browser and never uploaded.

How to use

1Choose a PDF
The tool shows how many pages it has.
2Set the range
Enter the first and last page you want to keep.
3Split
A new PDF is built with only those pages.
4Download
Save the extracted pages to your device.

Frequently asked questions

Can I extract a single page?

Yes — set both “From page” and “To page” to the same number, for example 5 and 5, and you get a one-page PDF.

Can I split into several separate files at once?

This tool extracts one range per run. To get another range, change the numbers and split again — the original file is untouched.

What if I enter a page number beyond the end?

The tool checks your range against the real page count and shows an error naming the valid range, instead of producing a wrong file.

Does splitting reduce quality?

No. Pages are copied exactly as they are — text stays selectable and images keep their original resolution.
