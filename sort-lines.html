Frequently asked questions

Is the sort case-sensitive?

No — sorting ignores case, so “banana” sorts with the B's rather than after all the capitalised words, the way most people expect a list to read.

How are numbers handled?

Naturally: the numeric parts compare by value, so “Track 2” sorts before “Track 10”. A purely alphabetical sort would put 10 first.

What is the difference between Z–A and Reverse order?

Z–A sorts the list alphabetically backwards. Reverse order does not sort at all — it flips your exact current sequence, keeping duplicates and grouping as they were.
