BMI Calculator

Body Mass Index from height and weight — a quick screening number used worldwide, with the standard adult categories.

BMI is a general screening guide for adults, not a diagnosis. It does not account for muscle mass, age or body composition — talk to a clinician about your individual health.

Frequently asked questions

What are the standard BMI categories?

For adults: below 18.5 underweight, 18.5–24.9 healthy weight, 25–29.9 overweight, 30 and above obese. These are the WHO ranges used by most health services.

Why does BMI mislead for muscular people?

BMI only sees height and total weight — it cannot tell muscle from fat. Athletes often land in “overweight” with low body fat, and older adults can be “healthy” on BMI while having low muscle. It is a first-pass screen, not a verdict.

Can I use this for a child?

No. Children's BMI is read against age-and-sex growth percentiles, not the adult categories shown here. Use a paediatric chart or your family doctor for anyone under 18.
