Frequently asked questions

Does it include area? Many converters skip it.

Yes — square metres, square feet, square inches, hectares, acres, square kilometres and square miles are all in the Area group, alongside length, weight, volume, speed and data.

Why can't I convert kilograms to litres?

Because that conversion depends on what the substance is — a litre of water weighs 1 kg, a litre of oil about 0.91 kg. Weight and volume are different families, so the tool refuses rather than inventing an answer.

How precise are the results?

Calculations use exact international definitions (1 inch = 2.54 cm and so on) and results are shown to up to 6 significant decimals — more than enough for everyday use.
