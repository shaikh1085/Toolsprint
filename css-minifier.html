Frequently asked questions

Is the minifier safe for my styles?

It removes comments, collapses whitespace and trims spaces around { } : ; and commas — the transformations that cannot change meaning. It does not rename classes, merge rules or drop properties, so your CSS behaves identically.

Why is my saving smaller than a build tool's?

Bundler minifiers also do riskier optimisations (merging selectors, shortening colours, removing overridden rules). This tool sticks to the safe subset — a smaller but guaranteed-equivalent win.

Can Beautify restore a truly minified file?

It restores structure — newlines and indentation — so the logic is readable again. It cannot restore comments or original formatting choices, because minification deleted those permanently.
