Frequently asked questions

Can I skip numbering on the cover page?

Not in this version — numbering starts at page 1. If your document has a cover, a common workaround is to split the cover off with PDF Split, number the rest, then merge the cover back in front.

What font and size are the numbers?

Helvetica at 10pt in medium grey, placed 22 points above the bottom edge — small enough to stay out of the way, dark enough to read when printed.

Will the numbers cover my content?

They sit in the bottom margin area. If your document's content runs right to the bottom edge, the number may overlap it — check the preview pages after downloading.

Can I remove the numbers later?

No — they are drawn into each page. Keep your original PDF if you might need an unnumbered copy.
