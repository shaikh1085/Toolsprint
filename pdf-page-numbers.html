Frequently asked questions

Can I use non-English letters in the watermark?

Only standard Latin letters, numbers and common punctuation. The built-in PDF font cannot draw other alphabets such as Arabic, Urdu or Chinese, and you will see an error message if you try.

Can I skip numbering on the cover page?

Not in this version, numbering starts at page 1. A common workaround is to split the cover off with PDF Split, number the rest, then merge the cover back in front.

Will the watermark or numbers cover my content?

Numbers sit in the bottom margin. A watermark is drawn over the page in a light, see-through colour so text underneath stays readable. Check a middle page after downloading.

Can I remove the stamp later?

No, it is drawn into each page. Keep your original PDF if you might need a clean copy.
