Why ToolSprint

Nothing is uploaded

Compression, merging and converting happen in your browser using your own device. There is no server copy of your file to leak, keep or sell.

No account, no watermark

Every tool is free to use in full. No sign-up wall, no trial countdown, and no watermark stamped on your downloads.

One honest job per page

Each tool has its own page with its own steps and FAQs, so search engines — and you — can find exactly the tool you need.

Deliberately not here: finance and tax calculators live on PayWise, and colour tools live on Chromora — ToolSprint stays a general tools hub. PDF-to-Word and video converter are not offered, because their in-browser quality is not good enough to promise.

Frequently asked questions

About ToolSprint itself.

How many tools are on ToolSprint, and how are they organised?

There are 51 tools in seven groups — Image, PDF, Text, Developer, Generator, Calculator and Timer. The ten most-used tools sit under Popular tools at the top of this page, and every tool also appears again in its group below, so you can either search or browse.

How does the search on this page work?

Type part of a tool's name or the job you want to do — for example “PDF”, “QR” or “password”. Matching tools appear under “Search results” as you type. Clear the search box and the Popular tools and grouped sections come back.

Do I need to install anything or create an account?

No. There is nothing to install and no account, sign-up or trial. Open a tool page in any modern browser on a phone or computer and it works straight away, with no watermark on anything you download.

Why are there no finance or colour tools on ToolSprint?

On purpose. Finance and tax calculators live on PayWise, and colour tools live on Chromora, so each site keeps one clear job. ToolSprint is the general tools hub — PDFs, images, text, developer and everyday tools only.
