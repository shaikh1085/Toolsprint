Frequently asked questions

Is a drawn signature like this legally binding?

That depends on your country and the document. Many places accept electronic signatures for everyday agreements, but for legal, banking or government documents, check what signature type they require first.

Why is the background transparent?

So the signature can be placed over a signature line or document background without a white box covering the text underneath.

How do I sign more neatly on a phone?

Turn the phone sideways for a wider canvas, zoom the page in, and draw slowly — or use a cheap stylus for much better control than a fingertip.
